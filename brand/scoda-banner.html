How to print / export this banner:
1. Press ⌘ P (Chrome recommended).   2. Destination: Save as PDF.   3. Layout: Landscape.   4. Paper size: A3 (or A2/A4 — it scales perfectly).   5. Margins: None.   6. ⚠️ Turn ON “Background graphics” (under More settings) — this is what keeps the black background & gold.
For a giant wall print, send the saved PDF to a print shop and ask for A1 or A0 — it’s vector, so it stays razor-sharp at any size. This box does not appear in the print.
