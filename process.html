How We Work

How We Build.

Every Templ project follows the same disciplined process. Here is what it looks like to build with us.

1

Step One

First Contact

It starts with a conversation.

Face to face is always the preference. A phone call or video works just as well. The point is simple. Before anything else happens, we sit down and talk. No forms. No automated responses. Just two people working out whether this is the right fit.

That conversation matters more than most people realise.

2

Step Two

Design & Pricing

We work out exactly what you need.

If your plans are finished we get straight to it. If you need help finding the right designer we can point you in the right direction. From there we price the project properly based on the scope and the detail it deserves. There is a quoting fee for this stage. It comes off the build price when you proceed.

Everyone has skin in the game from the start.

3

Step Three

Pre-Construction

Everything is prepared before a single tool is lifted.

The contract is written collaboratively. The scope is defined together. Insurances are supplied and permits are applied for and managed on your behalf. Nothing moves forward until every box is ticked and both sides are completely aligned.

This stage protects everyone. It is not something we rush.

4

Step Four

The Build

This is where Templ earns its reputation.

Our onsite team operates to a standard most people have not experienced on a residential project. Trades and timelines are managed through WunderBuild and you are kept across everything through your own client portal. Weekly site visits are encouraged. Milestone payments are documented and agreed before each stage.

Clients consistently tell us the same thing. They cannot believe the pace. They cannot believe the precision. They never once wondered what was happening.

5

Step Five

Handover & Aftercare

We hand over a home. Not just a build.

The formal handover process is followed by a defects inspection and a defects liability period in line with standard industry requirements. Every client receives an aftercare handbook with a clear and simple guide to making warranty claims.

Most builders disappear at this point. We make sure the next two years are as seamless as the build itself.

6

Step Six

You Enjoy Your New Home

This is what it was all for.

The plans. The permits. The decisions you did not expect to make. The early mornings. Every part of the process existed to get you to this moment. Standing in a home that was not there before. Built exactly the way you imagined it. By people who genuinely cared about it as much as you did.

That part never changes. No matter the size of the project.

We do not just build homes. We build the place where your life happens next.
