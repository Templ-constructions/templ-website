Rhyll — Phillip Island

Rebuilt From the Storm

Residential Renovation & Extension

This one is personal. The old place was Mum's little beach shack, and when the flood went through it was damaged beyond saving. Instead of patching it up, we made the call to rebuild it properly, tying the new build into the tower and bungalow that survived. Five bedrooms, three bathrooms and a central deck where the whole family gathers, looking straight out at the water with nothing in front of it. We built Mum her dream beach house.

Richmond

A Place for Men to Heal

Commercial Conversion — Design by Lauren McKillop

A brick terrace converted into a fully soundproofed psychology practice, built specifically as a space where men feel safe enough to do the work. Warmth, function and acoustic integrity drove every decision: honed marble at the front desk, microcement bathrooms, every room made to welcome rather than intimidate. Lauren McKillop's design deserved careful hands, and that's what it got.
